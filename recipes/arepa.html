Arepas Recipe

Description

Arepas are round, flat cornmeal cakes from northern South America, traditionally made from ground maize dough and often stuffed or topped with a variety of fillings.

    Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups lukewarm water

  • 1 teaspoon salt

  • 2 cups pre-cooked white cornmeal

  • ¼ cup vegetable oil, or as needed

    Steps

  1. Stir lukewarm water and salt together in a medium bowl; gradually stir in cornmeal with your fingers until a soft, moist, malleable dough forms.

  2. Form dough into eight 2-inch balls; pat each flat into a 3/8-inch-thick arepa patty.

  3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Working in batches, fry arepas in hot oil until golden brown, 4 to 5 minutes per side. Transfer cooked arepas to a paper towel-lined plate to drain until cool enough to handle.

  4. Slice each arepa halfway through horizontally with a thin serrated knife to form a pita-like pocket.

  5. Fill with desired fillings and enjoy!
